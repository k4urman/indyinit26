Why it works

Scams move fast. Blocklists don’t.

Most scam pages are gone within hours, long before they reach a list. Sentinel looks at what a page is doing — impersonating your bank, rushing you, locking your screen — and steps in at the moment it matters.

What it catches

Real-time, on the page.

Fake sign-in pages

Look-alike addresses and pages pretending to be your bank, Apple, Microsoft or Google.

Delivery & toll fees

“Pay $1.99 to release your package” — the most reported text-message scam.

Fake virus warnings

Pages that lock your screen and tell you to call “Microsoft”. Sentinel breaks them.

Remote-access lures

Pauses AnyDesk / TeamViewer downloads a “technician” told you to install.

Fake CAPTCHAs

Stops “ClickFix” pages that copy a hidden command for you to paste and run.

Gift-card & crypto scams

Government imposters and “guaranteed returns” investment platforms.

Install

30 seconds, no account.

  1. Download the Chrome/Edge package above and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped sentinel-chrome folder.
  4. Pin Sentinel to the toolbar. That’s it — you’re protected.

Firefox: use the Firefox package → about:debugging → This Firefox → Load Temporary Add-on.
